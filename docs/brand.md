# Pop In brand sheet

One page for the app, the site (popin.tangomike.app) and the App Store pictures. Story 8.17 (GR-22, D-20).

## Ground and ink

| Role | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | White `#FFFFFF` | App, site, store pictures |
| Ink | `#141414` | Titles, body, buttons on white |
| Secondary | `#4A4A4A` | Second lines, captions (8.0:1 or better on white and soft pink) |
| Soft pink | `#FFF0F6` | Quiet panels and ghost buttons on the site |
| Pink words | `#C8146A` | Links, kickers and pink words on white (5.6:1 on white, 5.1:1 on soft pink) |
| Bubble pink | `#FF7FB0` | The bubble only; never words on white |
| Deep band | `#1E1620` | The site's Pro and price band only |

## The bubble

- The bubble is the only color mass. Everything else is white, ink and grey.
- Every ball, large or small, has the same light: a curved gloss streak on the upper left, a small window and a
  glint (8.5).
- Words never sit on the gum; they sit on white or on the app's white plate (at least 90% white).

## Type

- App: SF Pro. Home's countdown is the hero numeral (88 pt on iPhone, 110 pt on iPad, medium weight, tight tracking).
- Site: Geist as the web stand-in for SF Pro, at matching tracking (titles about -0.03 em).
- Plain words, the app's own names: Smart, Always rings, Quiet, Not going, Snooze 2 min, Pop the bubble to join.

## Store pictures

- Same white ground and ink. iPad pictures are landscape and upright (7.5).
- Show the app as used: the bubble, the countdown card, the alarm screen.
