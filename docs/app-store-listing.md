**Description:**
For people who get deep into their work and look up ten minutes late.

Pop In reads the calendars already on your iPhone and rings a real alarm before each meeting, even on silent or in Focus. A soft gum bubble grows as the meeting gets closer. When it's time, pop the bubble to jump straight into Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex and more. Not going? Tap "Not going" and it stays quiet.

When your phone is locked, the alarm fills the screen. When you're using your phone, it shows as a banner at the top; tap it for the bubble.

Running late? The bubble notices. More bubbles bud off until the whole screen is bubble.

**Review notes:**
Pop In reads the iPhone's calendars (read only) and sets meeting alarms with AlarmKit. On first launch it asks for Calendar (Full Access) and Alarms permission; please allow both. To see an alarm without waiting for a meeting: tap the gear (top right), then Test the alarm. It rings in 20 seconds. Lock the phone to see the full-screen alarm; with the phone unlocked, iOS shows the alarm as a banner, which opens the bubble when tapped. Pop the bubble to clear it. Apple Reminders access is optional (Settings, off by default) and also read only. No account or sign-in, no network use, no data collection. Pop In Pro is a one-time in-app purchase under the gear, Pop In Pro. The tip jar appears in Settings only after Pro is bought. Additional seasonal bubble themes become available on set dates through the year; they are cosmetic and included with Pop In Pro.

**What's New (4000):**
A missed alarm now keeps ringing.

• Missed it? The alarm keeps ringing until you dismiss it, the meeting ends, or your next meeting is about to ring.
• Double-booked? When two meetings start at the same time, Home lets you pick the one that rings. The other stays on Home with a Join button.
• Already on a call? If a meeting you joined is still running, the next alarm still fills the screen but rings more softly.
• Clearer when meetings pile up: answering an alarm also clears the earlier meetings it replaced.
• Changed your mind? Bring back a meeting you marked Not going until it starts.
• A cleaner Home that stays readable at every text size, with a bigger countdown on iPad.
• Widgets now say "8 min", like the app, and work with tinted and clear Home Screens.
