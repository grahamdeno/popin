Support

Need help with Pop In? Email support@tangomike.app and put "Pop In" in the subject. Graham D. reads every message.

The alarm didn't ring

  • Open Settings > Pop In and make sure Calendars is set to Full Access and Alarms is on.
  • In Pop In's settings, check that the calendar is switched on and set to Smart or Always rings. Smart rings for meetings with a video link; other events get a quiet notification. Quiet sends notifications only. Always rings and Quiet come with Pro.
  • Open Pop In once after adding a meeting on another device, so it can set the alarm.

Why does the Lock Screen alarm look like Apple's?

On a locked iPhone, iOS draws every alarm screen. Pop In sets the meeting name and the color, and the buttons: Dismiss, and Join with the meeting's service (for example Join Zoom), or Open when the meeting has no link. When Pop In is open, it shows its own full-screen bubble instead.

Which calendars work?

Any calendar on your device: iCloud, Google, Outlook or Exchange accounts added in Settings > Calendar > Accounts.

Which meeting apps?

Zoom, Microsoft Teams (including government clouds), Google Meet, Webex, GoTo, Amazon Chime, Whereby, Slack huddles and about twenty more, from links in the event's URL, location, or notes.

Why is the alarm a banner when I'm using my phone?

That's how iPhone shows every alarm, Apple's Clock included: full screen on the Lock Screen, a banner at the top when the phone is unlocked. Tap the banner for the bubble.

The app icon didn't change with my theme

Settings > Theme shows which icon your Home Screen has, with a button to switch it. If iOS refuses ("Resource temporarily unavailable"), restart your iPhone and try again; that clears an iOS glitch.
